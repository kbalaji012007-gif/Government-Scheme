# 🌾 RuralConnect Karnataka

### ರೂರಲ್ ಕನೆಕ್ಟ್ ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ — Government Scheme Discovery & Accessibility Platform

**Making government schemes easier to discover, understand, and access for every rural citizen in Karnataka.**

RuralConnect Karnataka is a bilingual web platform designed to help rural citizens discover government welfare schemes, understand eligibility requirements, prepare the necessary documents, and find nearby government service centers.

The platform aims to make government information more accessible to farmers, students, women, senior citizens, and rural families through a simple, user-friendly experience in **English and Kannada**.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many rural citizens face difficulties accessing government welfare schemes because:

* **Limited awareness:** Citizens may not know which schemes are available to them.
* **Eligibility confusion:** Understanding scheme requirements can be difficult.
* **Language barriers:** Government information may not be easy for everyone to understand.
* **Documentation challenges:** Applicants may be unsure which documents are required.
* **Accessibility issues:** Citizens may struggle to find nearby government service centres.

These challenges can prevent eligible citizens from discovering and accessing benefits intended for them.

## 💡 Our Solution

RuralConnect Karnataka brings government scheme information into one accessible platform.

Users can explore available schemes, review eligibility criteria, understand application requirements, and locate service centers through a simple interface.

### Key Objectives

* Improve awareness of government welfare schemes.
* Make scheme information easier to understand.
* Help users assess their eligibility.
* Provide clear document and application guidance.
* Improve access to government service centres.
* Support both English and Kannada users.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🌐 1. Bilingual Interface

Designed to support users in English and Kannada, making government information more accessible to Karnataka's rural communities.

### 🔍 2. Government Scheme Discovery

Browse and search available government schemes to find relevant opportunities and benefits.

### 📋 3. Scheme Details

Explore scheme information, eligibility requirements, required documents, and application guidance.

### ✅ 4. Eligibility Assessment

A guided eligibility-checking workflow helps users assess whether they may qualify for a scheme.

Possible outcomes include:

* **Likely Eligible**
* **Not Eligible**
* **More Information Required**

Eligibility results are informational and do not constitute official government approval.

### 📍 5. Government Service Centre Locator

Help users discover relevant service centres, including:

* Grama One
* Karnataka One
* Bangalore One
* Bapuji Seva Kendras

*Centre availability and location information should be verified before visiting.*

The project includes an authentication component intended to support secure user access.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Visit the platform:** Open the RuralConnect Karnataka website.
2. **Explore schemes:** Browse or search available government schemes.
3. **Review requirements:** Read the eligibility criteria and required documents.
4. **Check eligibility:** Complete the available assessment to receive an informational result.
5. **Find assistance:** Explore relevant government service centres.
6. **Prepare to apply:** Use the available guidance to understand the next steps.

*The availability of individual steps depends on the features currently implemented in the live application.*

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

RuralConnect Karnataka is an informational platform intended to help citizens discover and understand government schemes.

It is **not an official government website** and is not affiliated with any government department unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Scheme availability, eligibility rules, required documents, and application procedures may change. Users should verify information with the relevant official government department or authorised service center before applying.

An eligibility assessment on this platform does not guarantee approval or benefits.
